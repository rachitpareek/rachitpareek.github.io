> Helping my brother apply to college over the last few months made me acutely aware of the immense amount of time and pressure involved in this process. ## Style Rules

When writing or editing, follow these rules:

1. **Voice**: Conversational, first-person, thinking-out-loud. Write as Rachit speaks.
2. **Qualifiers**: Use "I think", "I don't know", "at least until", "probably" naturally. Don't remove uncertainty.
3. **Parenthetical asides**: Use parentheses liberally for tangential thoughts, self-corrections, and clarifications.
4. **No em-dashes**: Use parentheses for asides, commas for pauses, or split into separate sentences.
5. **No filler sentence starters**: Don't start sentences with "So", "Well", or similar connectors (fine mid-sentence).
6. **Concrete details**: Ground abstract points in specific examples, numbers, or personal experiences.
7. **Open-ended endings**: Don't manufacture neat conclusions. If the thinking is genuinely unresolved, say so.
8. **Privilege acknowledgment**: When relevant, acknowledge position/privilege without being performative about it.
9. **Structure**: Use headings and lists when content demands it, but don't over-organize. Some sections should flow as prose.
10. **Data presentation**: When presenting data, use tables for comparisons and weave personal commentary between factual sections.
11. **Don't add content**: Only work with what's provided in notes/dictation. Don't invent facts or opinions.
12. **Preserve authenticity over polish**: Raw and real beats formally perfect.
